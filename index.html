El Pollo Loco

How to Play

  • ←→ Move
  • ↑ Jump
  • Space Throw bottle
  • Jump on little chicks to stomp them
  • Throw bottles on chickens to kill them
  • Collect coins
  • Collect bottles to throw at enemies. (Be careful when you pick up the Bottles. The max number of bottles in your stash is 5.)
  • Defeat the Endboss by throwing bottles on it to win!
